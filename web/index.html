How it works

Every step is a NEAR transaction anyone can check.

1. Fans vote

Vote 0.1 NEAR or more on a plot, or pitch your own for 1 NEAR.

2. AI writes

When the vote closes, NEAR AI writes a 4-panel script from the winning plot.

3. Art kit draws

Hand-drawn HOODL characters, scenes and props, picked by the AI.

4. NFT minted

The episode is minted on NEAR to the fan whose plot won.

5. Tips split

50% to the episode owner, 30% treasury, 20% AI reserve. Instantly.

6. Agent pays

It pays its own AI bill and sends 70% of each vote pot to the treasury.
